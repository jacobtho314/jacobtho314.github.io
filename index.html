Bio

I am a PhD student in the LTI at CMU, where I am fortunately advised by Yonatan Bisk. I am interested in augmenting LLMs with cognitive world models so they can:

  • learn from a few examples,
  • update their knowledge locally without forgetting what they already know, and
  • hallucinate less and offer formal guarantees about their reasoning.

Before CMU, I interned at Caltech’s Autonomous Robotics and Control Lab under Soon-Jo Chung, and was first introduced to research at MIT’s Computational Reactor Physics Group.
